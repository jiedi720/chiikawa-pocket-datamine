本计算器使用的公式（来自拆包数据）

单次伤害

damage = attack × hitRate × attackRate(costume) × bossOrMobRate × defenceDebuff ÷ attackDebuff
crit (one roll < critRate): × critDamage × critDefenceDebuff
expected crit multiplier = 1 + critRate × (critDamage − 1)

单槽位 DPS

normalDPS = attack × normalRate × costumeAttackRate × swingsPerSec
skillDPS  = attack × skillMult(skillLv) × skillCoef × costumeAttackRate × skillRate × uniqueRate ÷ cycleTime
cycleTime = recast × (1 − cooldownReduction) + castTime + 0.5s queue
skillMult = lerp(startValue → endValue over level range) ÷ 1000  (Permil)

增益 / 减益（作用于全队）

uptime = buffDuration ÷ cycleTime (≤ 1)
attack/crit buffs & defence-debuffs act as ×(1 + value × uptime) team multipliers

站位顺序

Your team shares ONE HP pool — the party wipes together when it empties; nobody tanks or dies early.
Single-target hits land on the leftmost ENABLED slot (the lead) but drain the shared pool.
Order only decides skill fire sequence: FIFO in formation order (unique 1/0.5s, assist 1/2s).
Buffs/debuffs must be LEFT of your nuke to affect it. CP = HP×0.6 + Σ(normal×500 + skill×500) — rough estimate.

全队共享同一个体力池 —— 体力见底时全队一起倒下，没有人需要抗伤、也不会有人先倒下。单体伤害落在最靠左的「已启用」站位（即开团位），但会消耗共享体力。站位顺序只决定特技发动顺序：按站位 FIFO 依次发动（服装特技间隔 1/0.5 秒，支援间隔 1/2 秒）。增益／减益必须位于输出位的左侧才能生效。战力 CP = 体力×0.6 + Σ(普攻×500 + 特技×500)，仅为粗略估算。

标签协同使用可配置权重（默认每多一名成员 +2%）—— 游戏按标签数量生效，但各标签的具体数值由条件决定，请当作估算。「特技再发动缩短」（RecastReset）效果会标注但不计入评分。
